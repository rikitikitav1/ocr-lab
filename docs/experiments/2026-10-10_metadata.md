# 2026-10-10 - Metadata

Lead: metadata reads a book's title and copyright pages with scripts first and a local model after, to
tell books apart and find the toc. The word lists in the scripts work for the languages they were written
for and silently fail on the rest. The measurements ask what it costs on the checked fields to keep only
language-free signals and let the model answer the rest, and which neighbouring rules need the same
treatment.

## Setup

- **Gold** `eval/gold/metadata.yaml`: fields checked by hand on the book's own pages. 12 books, 96
  checked fields at first; extended by books 71 and 94 to 14 books, 108 fields.
- **Shelf**: every changed field over all books (107, later 117 recollected, jobs 3087-3203; the layer
  rule over the 202 files on the stand) listed with its old and new source and read by eye.
- Metadata is for telling books apart and finding the toc; it is not a catalogue, and the gold checks
  only fields on the book's own pages. Gates were written before each run.

## Changes

The table above carries the numbers; the subsections keep what a row cannot say: mechanisms, losers, reverts.

### Title pages without word lists

Before: 94 of 96 checked fields right. Fields won by word-list sources over the shelf: year 72, volume 40,
edition 32, authors from the PDF info 18, publisher 7. The change
(`app/stages/metadata/title_pages.py`, `app/stages/metadata/model_reader.py`): title-page patterns keep
only language-free signals (the ISBN, the catalogue card's punctuation, years next to ©); the edition,
volume, publisher, reissue, document version, platform and "this version" patterns go and the model
answers those fields; author names from the PDF info are split on `;`, `,` and `&` only. Gate: the 96
fields at tolerance zero, every field that turns wrong named with why; over the shelf every changed field
listed and read.

Second run (an edition history now needs two lines in a row): **the gate as written fails, 90 of 96 right**
(94 before). Turned wrong: book 3 edition 3 → null (the model answers none); book 5 document version 3.2.2
→ null; book 4 publisher No Starch Press → null; book 6 publisher Leanpub (from the platform phrase) →
null. Over the shelf:

| field | books moved | what |
|---|---|---|
| volume | 32 | most old values were page counts or series numbers read as a volume (855, 3640, 2006); the model now gives some as "Volume 4", "Band 11" |
| edition | 21 | old junk such as "2020" and "0" gone; four now differ from the model's answer |
| publisher | 7 | all now null |
| year | 3 | books 21 and 45 now agree with the model; book 55 lost 1986 ("Copyright 1986" without the sign) |

Two changes measured after it. **XMP and every file's info**: the XML metadata packet reads as plain text
in 65 of 117 books; every file's info dictionary is now read, the first usable value wins, rank unchanged
(lowest); checked fields 90 of 96 unchanged; book 116 got its title from XMP ("Персидско-русский
фразеологический словарь") over the model's garbage. **A file name by its shape**: the list of file
extensions that marked a junk value became a shape (one word ending in a dot and 2 to 5 letters or
digits); alone, it moved 13 authors, all "GoalKicker.com" on the Notes books, now empty (a site compiling
Stack Overflow answers, not a person).

Decision: the owner accepted the four losses (a reader who needs those fields can enter them by hand).
The model's volume is cut to its number. Default, with the XMP reading and the file-name shape; the
GoalKicker authors stay empty unless the owner overturns it.

### A text layer in a shifted encoding read as garbled

Book 116, a Farsi-Russian dictionary, has a text layer that maps Russian to phonetic signs (`ɨɛɟɫɱɟɫɬ`)
and Farsi to Latin-1 symbols, so metadata and every later stage got nonsense. Rule (`app/text_layer.py`): a page
whose letters are 30% or more phonetic-sign or private-use code points is garbled and goes to recognition
like a page without a layer. Over every page of the 202 files it fires on book 116 only: 546 of its 554
pages. An alternative, "letters mixed with symbols", separated worse: code books reach 0.25 on it.
Decision: default; the scan verdict counts garbled pages with pages without a layer once (see the run
and report experiment, picture pages).

### An edition equal to the volume is dropped

With the word lists gone, the model reads a series volume as the edition: book 71 got edition 4 from
"Volume 4" while the book prints "1st Edition 2018", and book 94 got edition 12 from "Volume 12" with no
edition printed. Rule (`app/stages/metadata/model_reader.py`): the model's edition is dropped when it
equals the volume the model read; a second proposed condition would have needed a list of edition words
and was not taken. Gold extended by books 71 and 94: 108 fields, 99 right before. Gate: every book's
edition listed before and after, only books whose edition equals their volume may move; expected 94
right, 71 still wrong, 100 of 108.

Editions moved on exactly three books: 71 4 → null (gold 1, still wrong); 89 2 → null (page 4 prints
"Volume 2", no edition, right by eye); 94 12 → null (no edition printed, right). No other checked field
moved: **100 of 108**, as expected. Decision: default.

## Where it stands

- Gold: 100 of 108 checked fields right on 14 books.
- Publisher is null on all 7 books that had one from the word lists; the model carries edition, volume
  and publisher alone.
- Open: book 71's "1st Edition 2018" and its series "Successful Dispute Resolution" (page 2) are not
  read; gold 94's title stops at the first subtitle, and whether it should is the owner's call.

## Caveats

- A book whose title pages the model misreads gets null where a word list once matched.
- The shifted-encoding rule names a mechanism, but the shelf holds one example of it (book 116), so a
  different shift (into Cyrillic or Greek letters, say) would pass unseen.
- The edition is compared with the volume as a string, so "4th" against "4" would slip through.
