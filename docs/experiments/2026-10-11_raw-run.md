# 2026-10-11 - A raw run of the shelf and its defects

Lead: before tuning anything further, run a declared sample of the shelf whole on the defaults, collect every
defect with the books it shows on, group the defects by mechanism and by how many books share them, and only
then fix the general ones. The question: on how much of each book does the bundle hold the book's text, and
which defects stand between the shelf and that.

## Setup

- **Sample**: 43 books, 37 with a text layer and 6 scans; Russian 15, English 20, Bulgarian 4, Romanian 2,
  Ukrainian, French, Farsi and Chinese one each (one book is English and Chinese). Books are named by their
  stand id, see [books.md](../books.md). Book 117 (Chinese and Russian) was cancelled: its language was read as
  Russian only and its toc fell apart into 165 one-page chapters, a book knob.
- **Runs**: the night runs 35-64 on the defaults, then runs 65-76, 77-95 and 96-98 (books 1, 4, 5, read fresh) on the code after the changes below, 99-100 (books 18 and 19, the two largest, 3087 and 1067 pages) and 129-131 (books 44, 50, 43, programming books, 2026-10-12),
  every book's last run on one code. The engines' readings are kept per page range and engine build, so the
  reruns only reassembled; no book was read twice.
- **Gates**: each change below had its gate written before it was measured, in the run log of the day. The
  finished runs were reassembled or rescored offline from their kept readings, with the change off and on.
- New report columns, measured from the start: `repeated_paragraphs` (a paragraph of 120+ characters met twice
  in one chapter), `out_of_order` (the share of 6-word runs, met once in the layer and once in the chapter, that
  sit off the longest run in layer order). See [measurement.md](../measurement.md).

## Changes

### The layer reader and words PDFium breaks

PDFium put a line break inside most words of book 8 ("Север\r\nе уступае\r\nт"), so the layer, which is
the gold side of lost and extra, held single letters. `app/pdf.py` now drops a generated break or space between
two letters on one row when the gap is under the row's tight gap plus a quarter glyph. Over all 117 books the
reader changed tokens on 8 only, and on 23 and 69 by subscripts read as one token, as the engines write them.
Next to a dash it joins only on 8, five times, all right ("как-т|о" -> "как-то").

### Spaced-out rows: reverted

The second half of the reader rule joined rows PDFium spaces out ("К Л И Н И Ч"), guarded by the joined word
occurring unbroken elsewhere in the book. On book 23 it read "the order s ú b d" (a vertex after a word) as
"orders", a content letter row joined; reverted from the default. It stays a book knob candidate for 11, 26,
77, 63 and 25.

### Titles with spaces taken out

`same_title` also accepts two titles that match with every space removed, shared by the subs measure and the
assembly's heading levels. Of book 11's remaining 66 missing subs, 53 are in the text but not set as headings
by the engines, 13 differ by the OCR layer's 0 for О.

### A shared page

Two chapters that meet on one page both took the whole page. Now the blocks before a chapter's own title on
its first page and from the next title on its last page go, titles sought only in short blocks with an exact
match first, and the shared page is scored over all the chapters that wrote on it. Book 16's remaining 14
duplicates come from a doubled toc entry ("Глава 3" once as a one-page entry, once as the chapter), not a
shared page.

### Engines and the card

- A layer glyph that reads as several characters crashed the table-mark count on book 23; each character is
  now looked at.
- A range MinerU has no GPU memory for is read again in halves, up to three times; only the failed half
  splits again, and the parts are kept as one reading (`app/engines/reading.py`, `config/engines.yaml`).
- MinerU gave its vLLM 79% of the 7.6 GB card by its own rule for small cards, leaving its layout models 33 MB;
  pages 223-244 of book 54 failed even in three-page halves. The stand now gives vLLM 60%
  (`MINERU_VIRTUAL_VRAM_SIZE`, docker-compose.yml); the same range then read whole, and three kept ranges of
  book 54 (two text, one scan) read byte-identical to their kept readings.
- The metadata carries `made_with`, the tools that wrote the files (PDF Producer and Creator), e.g. "ABBYY
  FineReader PDF" on book 9.

## Caveats

- `out_of_order` was added during this run, so runs before 61 have no value; the six books with values before
  the reruns were 73, 112, 80, 21, 20 and 54.
- The scans' agreement is not a share of correct text: it is how far two engines agree, and an engine that
  fails silently drags it down without the text being wrong (9).
- Book 110's layer is broken in the file itself (recognised by ABBYY FineReader Engine 11, rewritten by Ghostscript):
  spaces inside words ("otom ană", "porţ i",
  "m ai"), 40% of its layer words one or two letters long, and an invisible alphabet run on each page. Every
  fragment counts as a lost word and every whole word of the bundle as extra. Compared as 6-character runs with
  spaces and diacritics taken out, lost is 0.137 and extra 0.098 instead of 0.42 and 0.37. The rest is real: Docling
  took 299 of its layer pages by OCR of the picture and lost the diacritics ("Dupa eliberarea asezarilor"), and
  MinerU set LaTeX on junk pictures. Pages with drawings explain at most 0.02. Book 111 does not share it.
