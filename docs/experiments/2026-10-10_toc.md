# 2026-10-10 - The table of contents

Lead: the toc stage must give every book its chapters on the right pages, with levels and roles, from
whatever the book holds: bookmarks, a printed contents, chapter files, numbering in the text, or a
recognised contents on a scan. The measurements ask, source by source, which rule finds more chapters
on the right page without losing one, first with the bookmarks as gold and then on an outside set.

## Setup

- **Shelf with bookmarks off**: 78 books that have bookmarks (the 59 of the first shelf plus law books
  60-109 and books added on the day), collected without the bookmarks and scored against them
  (`POST /v1/eval/toc`, `bookmarks_off`). A bookmark chapter counts as found when the toc has it on the
  same page; an extra row is one the bookmarks lack. The evaluator's totals leave out books without a
  printed contents.
- **HiPS**: 49 open English law books with tables of contents written by people (48 scored, one PDF is
  missing), pinned at a fixed commit, fetched into `data/gold/hips/`, never committed
  (`"set": "hips"`).
- **Recognised books**: the 9 books whose contents come through recognition (6, 13, 15, 16, 110, 111,
  117, 118, 119).
- Every gate after the first two entries was written before its run. Changes that need real tocs are
  checked by recollecting the whole shelf against a snapshot of every stored toc.

## Changes

The table above carries the numbers; the subsections keep what a row cannot say: mechanisms, losers,
reverts.

### Reconciling the sources (2026-10-09)

The first version took the first source that had a contents. Before, by chosen source (41 books with
bookmarks of 59): bookmarks 41, printed contents 8 (two scans read by MinerU), chapter files 2, whole
book 6, stopped 2. Built (`app/stages/toc/reconcile.py`): the base is the printed contents when the text
confirms over half of its pages, else the bookmarks, else chapter files; numbering only votes; other
sources match base entries by title (similarity 88) within 15 pages, and an entry the base lacks is added
when two other sources have it; titles and pages from the base, levels from the bookmarks where an entry
has one (this fixed 20 books whose printed levels collapsed the top; GoalKicker's book 33 had 2 chapters
of 110 at the top); the end of the book is searched only when the start has no contents. Guards found on
the way: an index at the end read as a contents on books 46, 52, 53; a contents running head with a folio
read as an entry on books 54, 46. The 14 extra entries include the three parts of the Redis book.
Decision: default; no gate was written before the run. The printed page wins a conflict with the
bookmarks; later checks found it wrong in most such conflicts (change B).

### HiPS as an outside gold (2026-10-09)

First run, 470 of 631 top chapters. The 161 misses: 106 in the first 5% of pages (covers, series pages,
forewords, abstracts, the contents page itself, which HiPS counts as chapters), 3 in the last 5%, 52 in
the body (13 the same chapter written otherwise, 15 on another page, 6 seen by numbering but dropped
because it only voted, 18 seen by no source). Decided by the owner from that list: pages before the first
entry become a front matter section; the score compares titles by their words too; numbering may add a
labelled part or chapter alone. Then two changes alone on both sets (on the shelf neither moved a number,
1526 of 1559 found, extra 7): extra counted at every level (book 85 lists chapters at level 3), and bold
setting a line apart only when the body is not bold (12 false parts removed in 11 books, 66, 83, 89, 90
and others, where a font reports every weight as bold). Both default; no gate was written before. The
denominator went from 631 to 609 without a breakdown in the notes, so the two HiPS figures are not one
series. What is left of extra is mostly front matter bookmarks the gold lacks (Cover, Half Title Page on
book 81) and junk on book 89.

### Change A: numbering as the base when nothing else holds the book

When no base was chosen, labelled parts and chapters from the text become the toc if there are at least
`numbering_base_min` (3) (`app/stages/toc/collect.py`, `config/toc.yaml`). Jobs 3063 against 3060. Gate:
fires only on the 14 class A books; other books' numbers stay. Result: the other 70 books not a number;
Kafka 0 → 14 of 20, Learning Python 0 → 42 of 55, book 7 whole book → 9 of 10, the three Bulgarian novels
0 → 17, 21, 10. Book 64 now has its four parts (its chapters are unnumbered, invisible to numbering); 66
has chapters 1-6 as the bookmarks but scores 0 of 1 because its outline's top level is a lone "Cover
page". Guards found on the way:

- Labelled entries must rise (a tenth of the steps back tolerated) and must not open on neighbouring pages
  (under three tenths of the steps): book 81's "PART ONE/TWO/FOUR" sat on pages 7-9, a contents listing,
  out of order; 81 stays without a toc.
- An out-of-turn entry (outside the longest rising run of its kind) is dropped and named in the evidence
  (Learning Python's "Chapter 37", a cross-reference; book 18: 45 labelled). Neighbours are counted
  within parts and within chapters.
- A one-letter "C." or "V." is a letter in an A. B. C. run unless a longer Roman numeral is in the list;
  a number is digits, a Roman numeral of two letters or more, one set off ("I.", "Part I:", "Глава V") or
  one after a repeated word ("Часть I"); "What I Learned" and "C Programming" are not. Front roles over
  the shelf identical, the 9 recognised books not a row.

Outside the gate: book 15 (L'art grec, 84 pages) one chapter → CHAPITRE I, II, III on pages 15, 33, 55
(page 33 opens chapter II by eye). Books 12, 14, 28 and 29 stay whole-book (0, 2, 0, 0 labelled); 9, 13
and 55 are scans, where numbering has no text. Decision: default.

### Change E: service chapters on both edges of the numbered run

Owner's decision: keep preface, introduction, conclusion, bibliography and index as chapters, marked
service. `role=front` becomes `role=service`, given before the first numbered chapter and after the last,
unless more than 15% of the book's pages follow it; nothing is marked when numbering starts after the
first 15% of the pages. A lettered run counts as numbered (consecutive capitals after the same word or
none, or a lone "<word> A"). Jobs 3086 against 3084 (3085 failed on an empty toc in the scorer, fixed).
The rule writes the role, so the gate used columns it does not touch: found and extra must not move, a
new column `extra_service` counts extra rows service by position, and the firing list is read by eye.

Back edge: 106 chapters in 53 books; the 13 lettered appendices of books 30-42, 50 and 54 stay body.
Found 1841 and extra 49 unmoved, no book moved; `extra_service` 17 of 49 (47, 52, 71 five times, 78, 79,
80, 86 twice, 87, 97, 101). Expected service but not: 68 and 96 (Abstract, Acronyms; first numbered
chapter at 13 of 72 and 47 of 80, past 15%), 75 ("7 Greece" first numbered at 126 of 292), 74 (its toc
holds only its two tables). After review: book 104 also moved (its A.-E. run now opens on page 31; before
only "C." read as a Roman numeral, so no roles) and "Bibliography 655-676" became service, right by eye;
the lone "<word> A" form now needs a colon or the end of the title after the letter, so an author's
initial (book 88, "Pedro A. de Miguel ...") is not a number; "Appendix A:" still counts; no firing moved.
Decision: default; each chapter carries `role=service|body` in its opening comment and `report.md` lists
service chapters with sub-entry counts.

### Roman numerals in one module

Fifteen places in six modules held their own pattern, mostly `[IVXLC]{1,7}` case-insensitive, which takes
"Civil" for a number ("Civil Society" lost its first word). `app/numerals.py` holds one well-formed
pattern in one case ("XIV", "xiv"; not "Xiv", not "Civil"), a chapter variant stopping at C (so "D." in
"D. Conclusion" is a letter), an upper-case variant for numbered titles, a variant for contents lines and
page edges, and conversions. Reverted tries: a short-key filter in page placement (9 chapters placed a
page after their first section); a capital required for a number in words (book 64 lost its four
lower-case parts). A pattern with D and M and upper case only moved 71's and 97's "D. Conclusion" to a
wrong page and added "part ii" rows on 46 and 77, fixed by the chapter variant and the one-case rule. The
module as kept moved 2 books: 89 "Civil Law" 294 → 296 (right, the title is on 296); 87 one junk row "Ill"
(a layer's "III" in mixed case, which the old pattern read as a number by luck). Decision: kept; one loser
named, book 87's "Ill".

## Caveats

- The bookmarks are not a perfect gold: they carry their maker's choices (book 57's index letters, front
  matter the printed contents does not list), and they do not see the printed source getting worse on 94.
- The first reconciliation and the two HiPS changes had no gate written before the run. Change B was kept
  as a default before its effect on real tocs was seen (101 came out worse). A toc's input hash names only
  the knobs a book sets, so a changed default of a toc knob refreshes no stored toc; the shelf has to be
  recollected by hand.
- A book without a printed contents is left out of the totals, so change A moves no total; its effect is
  listed per book. Unnumbered chapters are invisible to it (book 64), and it reads the text layer, so
  scans do not get it.
- Known losses of the service rule by position: book 79 "Annex" and book 103 "Appendix" (unlettered).
  Doubtful: book 4 "Exercise Hints" (19 sub-entries), book 88 "Intellectual Property", book 11 "Т есты".
  Numbering that opens after 15% of the book marks nothing on either edge. The scorer's service rows
  count unnumbered parts as chapters where the toc folds them into their own pages.
- A level reader by style is only as good as the styles it has met; a single-number recognised contents
  page is accepted, so a stray one-entry page can still read as contents.
- `chapter_label` reads two shapes (first layer lines on a text book, top 20% blocks on a scan); only the
  scan shape is measured. Lessons 6 and 12 of book 119 hold an unlabelled review unit ("مرور دو") that
  stays inside the lesson before.
- "Part of Speech" stays a known edge of the numerals module: a number in words without a capital can read
  as a numbered part, since requiring a capital cost book 64 its four lower-case parts.
