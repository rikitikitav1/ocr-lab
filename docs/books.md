# The shelf

The books the stand is tried on. The number is the book's id on the stand; the notes and the
experiments name books by it. Titles, authors and years are what the stand's own metadata stage read,
so a wrong one is a finding, not a typo. The files themselves are not in the repository.

"made with" is what the files say wrote them (the PDF info Producer and Creator, the metadata field `made_with`): the
tool that wrote the file, which for a scan is not always the one that recognised its text layer. "structure from" is the source
the book's current structure took its base from (the report's `metadata.structure_sources.base`); bookmarks often give the
levels of a printed contents without being its base. "Used in" says which
measurements the book takes part in: every book's structure is collected; a book with bookmarks is scored with
them switched off; the metadata gold holds the books checked by hand; a full run is a whole book through
both engines to a bundle.

- **layer / garbled**: sampled pages with a usable layer, and with a broken one.
- **short words**: share of the layer's letter words one or two letters long. It depends on the language (the shelf's
  median is 0.20, Bulgarian books sit at 0.31 to 0.33 with sound layers), so it is read within a language: a layer
  broken by spaces inside words reads high (110: 0.40).
- **spaced rows**: share of lines with five or more single letters (letter-spaced headings, spaced-out text).
- **breaks per page**: line breaks PDFium puts inside a word on one row, which the reader joins (8: 61).
- **alphabet runs**: invisible runs like `tsrqponmlk...` in the layer.
- **pages with** JPEG, JBIG2, JPX, CCITT images and with a mask: a page built from masked layers is one Docling's
  default backend renders black when it has to recognise it (9).
